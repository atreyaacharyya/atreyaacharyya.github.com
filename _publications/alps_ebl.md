excerpt: |
  Light travelling across the Universe interacts with the extragalactic background light, a diffuse glow produced by stars and galaxies throughout cosmic history. We investigated whether hypothetical particles called axion-like particles could decay into additional background light and leave an imprint on gamma rays from distant active galaxies. Combining Fermi-LAT and H.E.S.S. observations of 11 blazars, we found no significant evidence for such a contribution. Instead, our results place strong new constraints on these particles and complement other observational searches.
